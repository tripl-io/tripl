## What you can do without asking

- Say that your product works with Tripl, integrates with Tripl, or is based on
  Tripl, as long as the statement is accurate. For example: "an Airflow
  operator for Tripl".
- Run unmodified Tripl, for yourself or your company, under its own name.
- Write about Tripl in articles, talks, and tutorials.
- Keep the name in the license notices and the copyright headers the code
  already carries.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a **modified** version under the name Tripl. A fork has to use a
  name and a logo of its own, and it must not suggest that the Tripl project
  makes or endorses it. Say "based on Tripl" instead.
- Offering Tripl, modified or not, as a hosted service under the name Tripl,
  for example "Tripl Cloud" or "Hosted Tripl".
- Using the name or the logo in a product name, a company name, or a domain.
- Selling merchandise with the name or the logo.

Questions and permission requests: open an issue, or write to
trademarks@tripl.io.
